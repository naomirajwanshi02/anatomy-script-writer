# Analogy objects, device screens and effects

Moved out of CLAUDE.md (section 5) so it loads only when a figure has an analogy. Everything in CLAUDE.md still applies (colours in section 3; lamps and wires in `rules/lamps-wires.md`).

- **The board is the base of every figure, always on screen; analogy objects stand on it.** When the analogy is an everyday object (a desk phone being dialled, a tap, a thermostat, a laundry machine), draw a recognisable isometric model of that object standing on the board, large enough to read at phone size, and let its own action carry the idea (keys pressed, its screen switching on). Small details a phone viewer cannot read (a small screen's text) are repeated in a callout plate raised on the board next to the object. The board may change shape at the fold (for example square for the analogy, long for the circuit), but it never disappears. Every analogy needs one strong colour pop in its opening frames (as water did in digital electronics), from the allowed colours below. Device screens switch on like the reference desk computer: a thin bright line opens vertically into the full screen, and switches off by collapsing to a line, then a dot. Colours stay the sepia palette of sections 2-3 only: neutral sepia inks and surfaces, rust #b5541c and light rust #f0a868 for active items, yellow #ffc21a only for lit lamps, blue #2f6fb8 for water or PMOS, red #c2402f for NMOS or warnings. Olive #4f5d24 is reserved for my iPad annotations, so figures never use it. A phone or device screen in an analogy is a backlit LCD in the series blue (light blue glass mixed from #2f6fb8 and paper, dark blue-black pixel characters) for contrast with the sepia; never the green Nokia tint (too close to my olive pen).
- **Effects that leave an object** (sound from a speaker, steam, air) start in front of the emitting part and spread outward, for example sound as ( o ) pairs of rust arcs around the woofer, each pair a little further out and fainter, stroke 5. Never thin arcs hugging a side edge, and never in a plane seen edge-on at azimuth 62 (use the front face's plane).
- Analogy figures (water, seesaw, runners) start with the analogy and fold into the original circuit; see `src/figures/latch-race-through-locks/` as the reference. Water in analogy figures is blue #2f6fb8 and turns into the rust signal as it folds into the circuit (the water kit's `liquidColor`).
