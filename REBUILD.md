## 10. Rebuilding the workspace from scratch (new machine)The source was not copied. What exists on the new machine: this file, `shorts/` (every built page; each `.html` is a self-contained minified bundle of React + the shared kit + that figure), `capture.mjs`, and `series/digital-electronics.csv`. The built pages are the reference: rebuild the source so a fresh build looks and loops identically. Work one figure at a time, in episode order, and only when I ask for that figure. Finished figures do not need rebuilding to make videos (`capture.mjs` renders the existing pages); rebuild an old figure only when I want to change it. New figures need only the kit plus one reference figure. Use `/new-figure <episode, idea>` (`.claude/commands/new-figure.md`) for new figures and the `figure-checker` agent (`.claude/agents/figure-checker.md`) to check them.**Setup.** Node 22+, Chrome, ffmpeg. `package.json`: `"type": "module"`, dependencies `react@18.3.1`, `react-dom@18.3.1`, devDependency `esbuild@0.25.12` (exact versions, nothing else). Put `capture.mjs` in `builds/`. One `"build:<slug>": "node builds/build-<slug>.mjs"` script per figure.**Build (`builds/build-figure.mjs`, `buildFigure({ entry, out, title })`).**- esbuild bundles `src/main-<slug>.tsx` with: iife, minify, jsx automatic, target es2022, `process.env.NODE_ENV` = "production", write false.- Write one HTML: `<!doctype html>`, `<meta charset>`, a viewport meta with viewport-fit=cover, `<title>` and `<meta name="iso-loop" content="<LOOP>">`. The loop value comes from bundling the figure's `model.ts` and reading its exported `LOOP`.- Inline the bundle CSS plus `src/page.css`; the body is `<main class="page"><div id="root"></div></main>` + the script + an inlined `src/iso/fit-plate.js`. Create the output folder if it is missing.- `page.css`: sepia paper (#efe3c8) root and body, box-sizing border-box, `.page{width:min(680px,100% - 24px);margin:40px auto 72px}`.- `fit-plate.js` crops a plate's SVG viewBox to its drawn parts (skips `.iso-halo`, glows and defs). The reel figures don't need it, but keep it for older pages.- Each `main-<slug>.tsx`: `createRoot(#root).render(<Figure/>)`, importing the figure, then `./iso/iso.css`.**Kit (`src/iso/`), reconstruct from any built page; the numbers below must match exactly.**- `iso-kit.ts`, projection:  - Types: Vec3 = [x, y, z] board units; z is up, +y is toward the viewer, +x is to the right.  - View: `{origin, scale, azimuth=45, elevation=30}`; figures use azimuth 62. With a = azimuth, e = elevation and k = scale·√1.6:    - `screenX = origin.x + (x·sin a − y·cos a)·k`    - `screenY = origin.y + ((x·cos a + y·sin a)·sin e − z·cos e)·k`  - `fitView(points, W, H, {pad, azimuth})` centres the projected points in a W×H stage.  - Solids: `prism(rect{x,y,w,d,r}, z, h, view, seg, bevel)` and `cylinder(cx,cy,r,z,h,view,seg,bevel)` return `SolidPaths {fill, outline, crease, top, shades[4], bevel?}` built from rounded-rect extrusions with a convex hull. The visible side faces get shade index by facing.  - Other helpers: `flatPath/flatRect/flatCircle` (board-plane paths at height z), `faceMatrix(p, z, view, axis)` (an SVG matrix that lays text flat on the board), `translateTo`, `heightPx`, `rectCorners`, `boxCorners`, `inset`, `prismFill`, `rectFrontEdge`, `pathD` (one decimal) and `solidSvg(paths, {tone, crease, inner})` (markup string for parts rebuilt every frame).- `react/draw.tsx`:  - `Solid{paths,tone,crease,inner,lit}` renders `.iso-solid` with `.iso-fill`, `.iso-shade[data-shade=i]`, `.iso-top` and `.iso-edge`.  - `Line{d,tone,flow,dashed}` renders `.iso-line[data-tone]`; `Dots{points,size,tone}`.  - `FaceText{transform,size,tone}` renders `.iso-face-text`.  - `Stage{width,height,label}` renders `svg.iso-svg` inside an in-view wrapper; `Plate` is the older card.- `react/live.tsx`:  - `useInView`, `approach(from,to,dt,tau,snap)`, and `useFrameLoop(tick(dt,reduced)→bool, active)`, which runs on requestAnimationFrame with dt ≤ 1/30.  - **Capture contract:** with `?capture` in the URL, the page has no real-time clock. A `<style>` disables all transitions. `window.__isoStep(dt, at)` calls every registered tick with dt, then pauses every `document.getAnimations()` and sets its currentTime to `at·1000`. `capture.mjs` depends on this.- `iso.css`: tokens per section 2; `.iso-line{fill:none;stroke-linecap:round}`; `@keyframes iso-flow{to{stroke-dashoffset:-32}}`, `.iso-line[data-flow]{animation:iso-flow 1.6s linear infinite}`; `.iso-halo{filter:blur(9px)}`; reduced-motion stops animations.- `entrance.ts`, all times in seconds:  - `ENTRY={contact:1.35, loopFrom:3}`, travel 1000 stage units.  - The board rises from t=0.6 with an ease of (1−u)^5.  - Parts fall from t=0.8 under gravity 2·1000/(0.55²), landing at 1.35 with restitution 0.09.  - Shake: decay e^(−9s) for 0.6 s, x = 3.5·sin(2π·13s), y = 6·sin(2π·8s+0.4).  - Readout reveal: r = (t−1.7)/0.6, applied as 1−(1−r)^4.  - `loopTime(t,loop) = t<3 ? 0 : (t−3) % loop`.  - Sparks: 3 per contact point, seeded Park–Miller (seed 11), speed 40–110, life about 0.45 s, gravity 220, 45 % "hot" (rust #b5541c, the rest light rust #f0a868).- `react/reel.tsx` and `reel.css`:  - `ReelScreen{className, readout}` renders `div.iso.<cls>.reel[data-figure]` > `.reel-centre` (first child at 95 % frame width) + `.reel-below`.  - `Sparks{points}`; `paintEntry(root, t, contacts)` transforms `[data-entry]` (shake), `[data-base]` (rise) and `[data-parts]` (fall), paints the sparks and sets `--readout-in`.  - In `reel.css`, `--fw = min(100vw, 100vh·9/16)` and `--u = --fw/1080`; the section 2 line weights are multiples of `--u`, and tables use font-size 0.042·fw.- `analogy/water.ts` and `water.css`:  - `liquidColor(u)`: #2f6fb8 → #f0a868 → #b5541c, never through grey.  - `waterBox(box, level, amp, t, phase, view)`: the surface z(x) = level + amp·(0.7 sin(0.55x − 3.1t + φ) + 0.3 sin(1.3x + 2.3t + 2φ)). It draws front, side and top faces plus two ripple lines and a rim, rendered by `waterSvg`.  - Also `glassBox`, `pipeX/pipeY`, `flowPath`, and `tap` + `tapSpokes(…, axis)`: one handle bar, along the pipe = open, across = shut.  - CSS: `.an-w-*` faces mix `--liquid` with paper #efe3c8 (62 % front, 42 % side); `.an-drops` are 4.2-wide dark-blue dashes 4/12 on `iso-flow`; `.an-glass` walls have 5 % shades.**Figure catalogue** (slug → page in `shorts/digital-electronics/NN …/`, loop length; each analogy figure folds into its circuit and back):| NN | circuit figure (slug, loop) | analogy figure (slug, loop 30 s) ||---|---|---|| 01 | binary-switches, 8 s | binary-switches-meter (bulb meter: 5-band vs 2-band meters, NOISE block, board truth table right of the lamps) || 02 | transistor-switch, 10 s | transistor-switch-tap (water tap) || 03 | basic-gates, 12 s | basic-gates-taps (taps) || 04 | nand-gate (universal NAND), 10.4 s | none (no analogy) || 05 | cmos-inverter, 10 s | cmos-inverter-taps (two taps) || 06 | inverter-to-nand2, 14 s | cmos-nand-taps (taps) || 07 | nand-vs-nor, 11.6 s | nand-vs-nor-pipes (pipes) || 08 | cmos-idle-power, 40 s | cmos-idle-power-drip (dripping taps) || 09 | wider-is-faster, 22 s | wider-is-faster-pipes (pipes) || 10 | glitch-hazard, 27 s | glitch-hazard-runners (runners, one gate delay = 2 s) || 11 | sr-latch, 20 s | sr-latch-seesaw (seesaw) || 12 | sr-latch-invalid, 10 s | sr-latch-invalid-seesaw (seesaw) || 13 | d-latch, 48 s | d-latch-lock (canal lock) || 14 | latch-race-through (3 latches), 14 s | latch-race-through-locks (water locks → 2 latches; the reference analogy figure) |**How to rebuild a figure.**1.
